<a id="login-security-settings"></a>

### Login security settings
You can configure 2-step login in the login security settings.
Email verification, mobile phone verification, and Google OTP verification are supported.

<a id="select-login-security-settings"></a>

#### Select login security settings
![userguide_02_20230330](https://static.toastoven.net/toast/user_guide/userguide_10_202303.png)
After logging in, click the login account in the upper right corner of the homepage, and select **Login Security Settings** from the dropdown menu that appears.
You can view the current login security settings and change them by clicking the **Change** button.

- 2-step login settings
    - 2-step login disabled: The initial state where 2-step login security is not in use.
    - Log in after verification by email or mobile phone: Receive a verification code to your email or mobile phone number registered in your member information at login.
    - Log in after Google OTP verification: Verify using the Google OTP app at login.

<a id="log-in-after-google-otp-verification"></a>

#### Set up login with Google OTP verification
In the 2-step login settings, select **Log in after Google OTP verification** and click the **Save** button.
Complete the OTP verification setup steps in order.

1. Install the OTP verification app.
![userguide_03_20230330](https://static.toastoven.net/toast/user_guide/userguide_11_202303.png)

2. Add your NHN Cloud account to the OTP verification app.
![userguide_04_20230330](https://static.toastoven.net/toast/user_guide/userguide_12_202303.png)

3. Enter the verification code for your NHN Cloud account displayed on the OTP verification app screen.
![userguide_05_20230330](https://static.toastoven.net/toast/user_guide/userguide_13_202303.png)

4. Enter the OTP device name. (Only Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese, spaces, and numbers are allowed / maximum 10 characters)
![userguide_06_20230330](https://static.toastoven.net/toast/user_guide/userguide_14_202303.png)

When OTP verification setup is complete, the registered device appears in the OTP device list.
![userguide_07_20230330](https://static.toastoven.net/toast/user_guide/userguide_15_202303.png)

- Registration criteria
    - You can register up to 5 devices.
- Deletion criteria
    - If you have two or more OTP verification devices: You can delete an OTP verification device.
    - If you have one OTP verification device: You must add the OTP verification device or change the 2-step login settings to delete the device.

## Register a payment method
You must register a payment method to use NHN Cloud services.
However, if you have been invited as a member of an organization or project, you can use NHN Cloud services without registering a payment method.

To register a payment method, log in and click the login account in the upper right corner of the homepage, then select **Payment Method** from the dropdown menu that appears.
The following payment methods are available on NHN Cloud.
However, the available payment methods vary depending on the country you selected at the time of registration.

- Korea
    - Individual: PAYCO credit card, credit card
    - Business: PAYCO credit card, credit card, bank transfer
- Japan
    - Individual: Credit card
    - Business: Credit card

## Manage credits
Credits managed by NHN Cloud are issued free of charge for the purposes of service registration, rewards, events, and compensation.
One credit is equivalent to KRW 1.
You can check your received credits by logging in, clicking the login account in the upper right corner of the homepage, and selecting **Credits** from the dropdown menu that appears.
Credits are managed according to the following policies:

- Credits are non-refundable.
- Credits have an expiration date. They expire after the expiration date and are managed flexibly by accumulation type.
- Credits are provided to members in select countries who have registered a payment method.
- Only one credit account is allowed per mobile phone number or business registration number. If a member violates NHN Cloud's operating policies, credits may be revoked.
- When NHN Cloud usage charges are incurred, available credits are automatically applied to reduce the charges.
- If your NHN Cloud usage charges exceed your available credits, the credits are deducted first, and the remaining amount is charged as a fee.
- Welcome credits can be used to pay for services provided by NHN Cloud.
    - Excluded services: Notification, GPU Instance, Marketplace, and services that require a separate inquiry

## Withdraw from membership
<a id="apply-for-membership-withdrawal"></a>

### Apply for membership withdrawal
To withdraw from NHN Cloud membership, after logging in, click the login account in the upper right corner of the homepage, and select **Member Information** from the dropdown menu that appears.
Go to the **Member Information** page and click the **Withdraw from Membership** button in the lower right corner.
Review the membership withdrawal notice, then click **Withdraw from Membership**.
You cannot apply to withdraw from membership while you have active services.

<a id="verify-membership-withdrawal"></a>

### Verify membership withdrawal
After applying for withdrawal, the following information is reviewed and the withdrawal is processed.<br/>
If there are outstanding charges, the withdrawal is processed after payment on the automatic billing date (the 8th of each month).

<a id="complete-membership-withdrawal"></a>

### Membership withdrawal complete
When the withdrawal is complete, a confirmation is sent to the email address registered at the time of the withdrawal request.
When you withdraw from membership, all usage information is deleted and the deleted data cannot be restored.
