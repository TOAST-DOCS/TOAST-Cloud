## NHN Cloud > Trademark Guide > Overview { #nhn-cloud-trademark-guide-overview }

NHN Cloud trademarks refer to all words, service names, logos, symbols, and designs adopted to represent NHN Cloud's products and services and to distinguish them from those of other companies.
These guidelines provide NHN Cloud members and NHN Cloud partners with proper trademark usage instructions to prevent improper use of NHN Cloud trademarks that could damage or dilute our brand value.
If you have any questions about items not covered in these guidelines, please contact the [Customer Center](https://www.toast.com/kr/support/inquiry).
<br>

<a id="the-nhn-cloud-trademark-may-only-be-used-by-nhn-cloud-members-and-nhn-cloud-partners"></a>
### 1. The NHN Cloud trademark may only be used by NHN Cloud members and NHN Cloud partners. { #the-nhn-cloud-trademark-may-only-be-used-by-nhn-cloud-members-and-nhn-cloud-partners }

<a id="the-nhn-cloud-trademark-may-only-be-used-by-nhn-cloud-members-and-nhn-cloud-partners-member"></a>
#### Member

- NHN Cloud members refer to individuals or legal entities that have entered into a service agreement with NHN Cloud and have been granted the right to use the services.
- NHN Cloud members may use NHN Cloud's trademarks only for the following purposes:
    - To certify that an NHN Cloud member is using NHN Cloud services
    - To identify or promote services or content in which NHN Cloud services are used
- If you no longer use NHN Cloud services or withdraw your NHN Cloud membership, you must immediately stop using NHN Cloud's trademarks.

<a id="the-nhn-cloud-trademark-may-only-be-used-by-nhn-cloud-members-and-nhn-cloud-partners-partner"></a>
#### Partner

- NHN Cloud partners are members of NHN Cloud who have entered into a partner agreement with NHN Cloud and resell NHN Cloud services.
- NHN Cloud partners may use NHN Cloud trademarks only for the following purposes:
    - To verify their status as an NHN Cloud partner
    - For marketing purposes related to reselling NHN Cloud services to NHN Cloud members
- If the partner agreement expires or is terminated, you must immediately stop using NHN Cloud trademarks.

<a id="nhn-cloud-members-and-nhn-cloud-partners-must-comply-with-the-following-requirements"></a>
### 2. NHN Cloud members and NHN Cloud partners must comply with the following requirements. { #nhn-cloud-members-and-nhn-cloud-partners-must-comply-with-the-following-requirements }

<a id="nhn-cloud-members-and-nhn-cloud-partners-must-comply-with-the-following-requirements-correct-trademark-usage"></a>
#### Correct Trademark Usage

- You must display the appropriate trademark in the appropriate location and use the appropriate attribution statement.
- You must append ™ to our trademarks to indicate that they are being used as trademarks.
- You must not use any of our trademarks as your trademark, service mark, domain name, product name, or logo.
- You must not use our trademarks in modified or abbreviated form, or combine or integrate them with other trademarks.

<a id="nhn-cloud-members-and-nhn-cloud-partners-must-comply-with-the-following-requirements-trademark-use-is-prohibited"></a>
#### Trademark use is prohibited

- You must not imitate or fabricate our trademarks to use similar marks that could cause confusion with our trademarks.
- You must not use our trademarks i) as a common noun, ii) in connection with products or services that provide illegal content, or iii) display our trademarks on products or services that otherwise violate the law.
- You must not display our trademarks in a manner that could create confusion suggesting any business, organizational, financial, or contractual relationship with us.
