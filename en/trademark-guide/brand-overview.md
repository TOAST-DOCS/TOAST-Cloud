## NHN Cloud > Trademark Guide > Overview { #nhn-cloud-trademark-guide-overview }

The NHN Cloud trademark refers to any words, service names, logos, symbols, and designs adopted to represent NHN Cloud products and services and to distinguish them from those of third parties.
These guidelines provide NHN Cloud members and NHN Cloud partners with proper trademark usage guidelines to prevent the NHN Cloud trademark from being misused in ways that could damage or dilute the value of our brand.
If you have any questions about topics not covered in these guidelines, please contact the [Customer Center](https://www.toast.com/kr/support/inquiry).
<br>

<a id="the-nhn-cloud-trademark-may-only-be-used-by-nhn-cloud-members-and-nhn-cloud-partners"></a>
### 1. The NHN Cloud trademark may only be used by NHN Cloud members and NHN Cloud partners. { #the-nhn-cloud-trademark-may-only-be-used-by-nhn-cloud-members-and-nhn-cloud-partners }

<a id="the-nhn-cloud-trademark-may-only-be-used-by-nhn-cloud-members-and-nhn-cloud-partners-member"></a>
#### Member

- NHN Cloud members refer to individuals or legal entities that have entered into a service use agreement with NHN Cloud and have been granted the right to use the services.
- NHN Cloud members may use NHN Cloud's trademarks only for the following purposes:
    - To verify that an NHN Cloud member is using NHN Cloud services
    - To identify or promote services or content that use NHN Cloud services
- If you no longer use NHN Cloud services or withdraw your NHN Cloud membership, you must immediately stop using NHN Cloud's trademarks.

<a id="the-nhn-cloud-trademark-may-only-be-used-by-nhn-cloud-members-and-nhn-cloud-partners-partner"></a>
#### Partner

- An NHN Cloud partner is a member of NHN Cloud who has entered into a partner agreement with NHN Cloud to resell NHN Cloud services.
- NHN Cloud partners may use the NHN Cloud trademark only for the following purposes:
    - To certify their status as an NHN Cloud partner
    - For marketing purposes to resell NHN Cloud services to NHN Cloud members
- If the partner agreement expires or is terminated, you must immediately stop using the NHN Cloud trademark.

<a id="nhn-cloud-members-and-nhn-cloud-partners-must-comply-with-the-following-requirements"></a>
### 2. NHN Cloud members and NHN Cloud partners must comply with the following requirements. { #nhn-cloud-members-and-nhn-cloud-partners-must-comply-with-the-following-requirements }

<a id="nhn-cloud-members-and-nhn-cloud-partners-must-comply-with-the-following-requirements-correct-trademark-usage"></a>
#### Correct Trademark Usage

- Display appropriate trademarks in the correct locations and use appropriate attribution statements.
- Mark our trademarks with ™ to indicate that they are being used as trademarks.
- Do not use any of our trademarks as your trademark, service mark, domain name, product name, or logo.
- Do not use our trademarks in modified or abbreviated form, or combine them with other trademarks.

<a id="nhn-cloud-members-and-nhn-cloud-partners-must-comply-with-the-following-requirements-trademark-use-is-prohibited"></a>
#### Trademark use is prohibited

- Do not imitate or falsify our trademarks, or use similar trademarks that could be confused with our trademarks.
- Do not use our trademarks i) as a common noun, ii) for products or services that provide illegal content, or iii) on products or services that otherwise violate applicable law.
- Do not display our trademarks in a manner that may create confusion suggesting any business, organizational, financial, or contractual relationship with us.
